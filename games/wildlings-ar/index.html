Field method

How to look

1. Go outdoors and place one real object inside the circle. The lens knows leaves, rocks, bark, moss, pinecones, acorns, mushrooms, water, clover, shells, clouds, reeds, bricks, snow, ash, points of light, and eight kinds of flowers.

2. Fill the object zone and hold still for four matching readings. The real object becomes the clue and transforms into a matching creature.

3. When the Wildling runs, tap it three times before it escapes. Once caught, tap the heart to become friends.

The recognition model downloads on first play, then analyzes small camera crops on your device. Camera images are never uploaded. Location is rounded into a broad habitat cell and is not stored or sent anywhere.
