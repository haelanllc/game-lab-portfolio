Field method

How to look

1. Allow location to build a nearby lineup from your latitude, climate band, hemisphere, and season. Exact coordinates stay on your device. You can still browse all 105 Wildlings, organized by habitat.

2. Every Wildling has a different real-world clue. Look for exact objects such as an oak leaf, quartz crystal, birch bark, chanterelle, cattail, scallop shell, icicle, or charcoal.

3. Fill the precision zone and tap Take photo. The lens freezes the picture, checks tight, natural, and context views, then identifies the object when two views agree. You can also leave it in live mode for four matching readings.

4. When the Wildling runs, follow its dodges and tap it three times. Catch a golden calm leaf for two extra seconds and a short slow-down. Once caught, tap the heart to become friends.

5. Choose Walk together beneath any friend. Keep the game open while you walk; every 250 meters raises that friend one level.

The recognition model downloads on first play, then analyzes small camera crops on your device. Camera images are never uploaded. Your raw location and route are never stored or sent; only each friend’s total walking distance is saved on this device.
