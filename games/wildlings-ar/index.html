How to look

1. Go outdoors and turn your camera slowly. The arrow points toward a hidden clue.

2. Hold the clue inside the circle. A leaf, bit of bark, flower, stone, cloud, or puddle will transform.

3. Tap the ＋ to befriend the Wildling. Walk somewhere new to meet a different local population.

The camera view stays on your device. Location is rounded into a broad habitat cell and is not stored or sent anywhere.
